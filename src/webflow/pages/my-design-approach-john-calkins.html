My design approach

I design with intent.

I kick off every project by asking questions. I chase clarity so I can balance business goals and potential constraints while still constantly advocating for the user.

I build systems, not just screens

I think in patterns: How can this UI scale? How will it evolve? What can we reuse and where do we need to rethink?

I think like a product owner

I don’t just hand off designs. I stay close to product and engineering through delivery and iteration.
